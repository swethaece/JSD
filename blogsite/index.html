Everyday Tech Tips

Plain-English guides to help you back up your files, protect your accounts, and keep your devices running fast.

Welcome to the Blog

Everyday Tech Tips is a small blog written by our team for anyone who wants clearer, jargon-free advice about the technology they already own. We are not trying to sell you anything — we just want fewer people to lose photos to a broken laptop, or have an account taken over because of a reused password.

Below are our most recent posts. Each one covers a single topic in a few minutes of reading, with a short checklist you can act on right away.

Latest Posts

Backups

Backup Basics: The 3-2-1 Rule

Keep three copies of anything you can't afford to lose: your working file, a copy on an external drive, and a copy stored somewhere off-site such as a cloud service. Most data loss we hear about could have been avoided with one extra copy.

Security

Password Hygiene in 5 Minutes

Reusing the same password across sites is the single biggest risk to your accounts. A password manager and two-factor authentication remove almost all of that risk, and take less time to set up than you'd think.

Maintenance

Speed Up Your PC Without Buying Anything

Before you spend money on new hardware, try trimming your startup programs, clearing temporary files, and checking for one runaway browser tab. It's a ten-minute job that fixes most "my computer got so slow" complaints.

Why This Blog Exists

Our group built this site as part of a hands-on web development project, but the goal behind it is a real one: technology advice is often written for people who already understand technology. We wanted a place that explains things simply, for people who just want their stuff to work. Read more about the team on the About Us page.
