About Everyday Tech Tips

We're a three-person team that came together for a web development group project — and decided the site should actually be useful once the assignment was over. Everyday Tech Tips exists because we kept noticing the same pattern: friends and family losing files, getting locked out of accounts, or living with a slow computer, all because of small habits that are easy to fix once someone explains them clearly.

This site is our attempt at that explanation. No affiliate links, no scare tactics — just short, practical posts you can act on the same day you read them.

What We're Doing, and Why

Each post on this blog is chosen because it addresses a real, common problem: a laptop that won't turn on with a thesis on it, an inbox taken over after a password was reused on a shopping site, a phone crawling to a halt after a system update. We research each topic, write it up in plain language, and keep it short enough to read in the time it takes to make a coffee.

Clarity First

If a tip needs a glossary to understand, we rewrite it until it doesn't.

No Fear-Mongering

We explain risks honestly, without exaggerating them to get clicks.

Actually Actionable

Every post ends with something concrete you can do in the next five minutes.
